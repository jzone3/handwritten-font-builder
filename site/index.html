Install the .otf like any font (double-click on Mac or Windows). It works in Pages, Word, Figma, Keynote, and on the web with @font-face. Letters you haven't drawn fall back to another font.

Better on a Mac

A browser only sees your trackpad as a mouse, so you have to click to draw. The free Mac app uses the trackpad like a drawing tablet: touch to write, lift to stop, no clicking. Write every letter with a finger, export one file, and drop it on this page.
