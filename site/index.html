Make a font out of your own handwriting.

You write each letter once, in a box on the screen. When you're done you get a real font file you can install anywhere: Pages, Word, Figma, your website. Nothing is uploaded; it all happens in your browser.

Click and drag to draw, or hold Space and move to draw without clicking. On a Mac, the app lets you write on the trackpad with a finger.
